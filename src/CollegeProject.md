# College Research

Research 10 colleges. For each one, answer:

- Why does this college interest you?
- What might you dislike?
- Where is it?
  - Distance from home
  - Campus and surrounding area
- Does it offer your possible major?
- What is student life like?
  - Campus size
  - Housing
  - Clubs and activities
- How hard is it to get in?
  - Acceptance rate
  - Typical GPA
  - SAT/ACT policy and scores
- What does the application require?
  - Courses
  - Essay
  - Recommendations
  - Other requirements
- What are the deadlines?
  - Early Action/Decision
  - Regular Decision
  - Financial aid and scholarships
- What will it cost?
  - Tuition, housing, meals, and fees
  - Estimated cost after financial aid
- Would you actually attend? Why or why not?
- Include links to your sources.

## Final Comparison

After researching all 10:

- Pick your top 5.
- Rank them.
- Give one reason for each ranking.
- List any questions you still need answered.
